The Vellum Vigil

How to play

The Veil has swallowed the realm. One mage remains above its last spire, armed with a blank grimoire. Survive 50 circles and defeat the five Archons.

Survive

Move and dodge. Your spells fire automatically.

Grow

Earn XP and choose boons to strengthen your magic.

Conquer

Defeat an Archon every ten circles.

The mage's field guide

Detailed mechanics for every vigil.

Combat and progression

Start with three lives. Ward Pulse damages nearby foes and clears projectiles (3.8-second initial recharge). Enemy score fills XP; every fifth cleared circle guarantees a boon. Maintain a kill chain within 2.4 seconds for higher scores.

Boons and gifts

Arcane Edge increases damage; Quickcast speeds casting; Swiftstep improves movement; Stormshot speeds projectiles. Ward Resonance reduces recharge and Expanding Ward increases range. Vitality raises maximum lives and restores one.

Falling gifts: Vitality restores a life, Surge speeds casting for 4.5 seconds, and Aegis absorbs one hit. Duplicate Aegis and full-health Vitality grant Surge.

Grimoire abilities and evolution

Choose Ashen Crown for fire, Hollow Winter for slowing ice, or Stormbound for piercing lightning. Q, E and R activate your family's three abilities with independent cooldowns. Two specialization upgrades make your evolution eligible for a later boon.

Enemies and Archons

Bats, imps, wraiths and cultists appear as circles progress. Defeat Vaelith, Mordrath, Elyndra, Tharos and Aurex at circles 10, 20, 30, 40 and 50.

Scores and achievements

Sign in to save runs and earn titles. Guests can preview their ranking after victory.
