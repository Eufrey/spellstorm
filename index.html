How progression works

You begin with the Basic Grimoire, which casts automatically. Move to dodge and gather score; score fills the experience meter and opens a boon choice.

Your first grimoire choice comes after Circle 5. Every five completed circles also brings a guaranteed boon choice.

Build toward an evolution. Choose two grimoire-specific upgrades. Its stronger form may then appear among later boon choices.
